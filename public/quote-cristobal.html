3. Artisan Hand-Applied Product Disclaimer

This floor is a hand-applied, custom-designed product created by an artist. Each floor is meticulously crafted by hand using specialized metallic epoxy techniques that involve hand manipulation of pigments, metallic powders, and resin materials. Due to the handcrafted nature of this product, there may be small imperfections, natural variations, and organic inconsistencies in the final result. These characteristics are inherent to the artistic process and are not considered defects. Rather, they add to the uniqueness, character, and one-of-a-kind nature of each custom floor. No two floors will ever look exactly the same — and that is by design. The client acknowledges and accepts this as part of the beauty and value of commissioning a hand-crafted, artist-designed floor.

4. Design Accuracy

The rendered design images provided are for reference and inspiration purposes. While Jake Epoxy / Resin Academics will make every effort to match the design as closely as possible, the hand-applied nature of metallic epoxy means the final result will be a unique interpretation of the design. Color tones, veining patterns, and metallic movement will be similar in style and palette but may vary naturally.

5. Project Timeline

The project is estimated to take 4–6 days depending on concrete condition, facility size, and environmental factors. A specific start date will be confirmed once the deposit is received. Jake Epoxy / Resin Academics will make every effort to complete the work within the agreed timeline, barring unforeseen conditions.

The floor area must be cleared of all personal belongings, furniture, equipment, and items prior to the scheduled start date. 7. Warranty

Jake Epoxy / Resin Academics warrants the installation workmanship for a period of 1 year from the date of completion.
