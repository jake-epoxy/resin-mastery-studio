Policy Terms & Conditions

This document outlines the standard operating procedures, observation boundaries, and media policies for all clients participating in the Jake Epoxy (Pour Masters LLC) observation program.

1. Observation Protocol

Jake Epoxy welcomes the Student Observer to watch the flooring installation process to earn their "Certificate of Observation." We value transparency and are pleased to provide this educational experience.

However, it is imperative to note that the installation schedule is strictly dictated by the chemical curing times of the resin and the operational requirements of the project. The Contractor cannot and will not alter the work schedule—including delaying applications or performing work outside standard hours—to accommodate the Student Observer's personal availability. Observation is permitted only during the naturally occurring phases of the project schedule.

2. Complimentary Media Gift

As a courtesy to our valued clients, Jake Epoxy provides a complimentary, fully edited "Before and After" promotional video detailing the transformation of the floor. This high-quality video will be fully branded with "Jake Epoxy" watermarks and logos. It is provided as a gift for the Client to share the transformation with loved ones or their own clients.

3. Strict Media & Intellectual Property Policy

The verbal agreement allowing the Client to "observe and get certified" does NOT include the transfer of any raw footage, unedited clips, or the rights to use the Contractor's likeness, proprietary installation processes, or unbranded footage for the Client's own commercial advertising or business promotion.

Any independent media captured by the Student Observer on-site must be strictly for personal, non-commercial use. Pour Masters LLC retains exclusive ownership of all intellectual property, techniques, and media rights associated with the installation process.

4. Commercial Media Partnership Buyout

If the Student Observer wishes to obtain raw footage, unbranded media, or commercial rights to advertise using our installation processes and likeness, they must apply for a separate Commercial Media Partnership Buyout. This buyout involves a distinct fee structure, a formal licensing agreement, and must be executed in writing prior to any commercial use of project media.
