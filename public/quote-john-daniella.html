Scope of Work
  • Full surface preparation — diamond grind entire concrete surface
  • Repair any cracks, pits, or imperfections in the substrate
  • Apply high-build tinted epoxy base coat
  • Broadcast premium decorative exterior vinyl flake to full rejection for UV/Slip resistance
  • Scrape and vacuum excess flake to create a smooth, even texture
  • Apply clear UV-stable polyaspartic/urethane clear top coat for long-lasting protection
  • Final walkthrough and quality inspection with client
